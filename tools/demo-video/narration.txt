When internet is unavailable, a person still needs a clear path to a responder. This prototype explores that local exchange.

The person selects a reported floor, reviews the sample SOS, and sends. The request is saved before transfer.

A relay stores an encrypted copy. It cannot read the private request, and custody does not confirm responder delivery.

The responder device saves the request before returning a signed receipt. Device delivery is not a human acknowledgment.

A responder acknowledges the request and sends a reply. Those are separate messages. Acknowledgment does not mean help was dispatched.

If a contact fails, the original remains saved. After restart, an operator reconnects and retries the exact encrypted message.

Twenty Mac loopback recovery scenarios confirmed sixty original messages. Physical radio behavior and private data readiness remain unverified.

The native phone and tablet share one C plus plus engine. Explore the code, evidence, and setup in the repository.
