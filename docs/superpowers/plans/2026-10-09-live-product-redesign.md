## Chosen design / critique
Replace tall navy diagnostic banner and nested generic cards with a compact connection strip, editorial native type, graphite canvas, one dominant request action and clear location/status hierarchy. Present updates/reply/handling in progressively disclosed sheets or compact action groups; maintain every previous action. Conversation becomes the primary active-request content. Responder uses a focused request detail with compact status/actions. Use real state-triggered transitions with Reduce Motion fallback; never animate a delivery fact into existence. Clear synthetic/training label persists.

Critique: orange alone must not carry status; text/icons still required. Layout changes must retain draft state. Keep error/recovery and unpaired/queued/stopped state visible. Avoid inventing a map, ETA, dispatch or sensor location. No model/transport changes. Existing paired secure samples remain untouched.
